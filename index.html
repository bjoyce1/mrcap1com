Mr. CAP

Mr. CAP (Cornelius A. Pratt) is a Houston-born rapper, writer, and creative entrepreneur, and an Original Member of the South Park Coalition alongside K-Rino, Klondike Kat, and Point Blank. He has been making music for over 30 years, and in 2021 he became the first Houston rapper to sell a hip-hop NFT.
