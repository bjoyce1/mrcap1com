## Scope and mode
The Today Was A Great Day (Remix) release world: `/greatday-preview` now (private, noindex), `greatday.mrcap1.com` at launch. Mode: Experience. The work leads from the first viewport.

## Audience, job, action
Now: Mr. CAP and team, the featured artists and their teams, partners and press reviewing the world before launch. At launch: fans arriving from the seven-day organic campaign. Job: hear the song, meet the four voices, add a great day, and later own a digital edition direct from CAP Distributions. Primary action: play.

## Proof and content
The original 2024 single plays as a 30-second preview until the remix master clears. The temporary cover sits in the pendant. Lyrics, cleared portraits and behind-the-record clips are owner-supplied slots in `content.ts`. Proposed edition prices and sample wall stories carry placeholder tags.

## Constraints
Payments, real signups, wall persistence and remix audio stay off until the publication gates in docs/greatday/SPEC.md close. One quiet private-preview strip; tags only on placeholders. No release date. Roster exactly Mr. CAP featuring Paul Wall, Lil Keke and Lisa Dinero.

## Direction and memorable moment
The Jeweler's Counter (src/features/greatday/DESIGN.md). Memorable moment: the cover hanging as a rendered gold photo pendant on a rope chain, with the case light sweeping across it and the nameplate title on load. The wall's engraver engraves whatever a visitor types onto a gold ID plate.
